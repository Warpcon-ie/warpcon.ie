You’ve ridden for days in pursuit of the raiders who stole your tribe’s cattle and murdered your kin. Sent by Zarah, the clan‑chieftain’s wife, you and your companions hunt the culprits—identified as Greydogs led by the bitter Danakos—toward their home territory in Lismelder lands, seeking to reclaim what was taken and deliver justice for the blood spilled. Bronze-age fantasy adventure with a gritty old-school flavour. Newcomers welcome, no system or setting familiarity required
