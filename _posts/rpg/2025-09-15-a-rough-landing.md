The city of Jonstown is in turmoil after the recent victory over the Lunar Empire.   Nothing except a battle lost can be half so melancholy as a battle won!  Some unruly trolls drag the adventurers into trouble, leading to the authorities assigning them a simple task... Simple?

Bronze-age fantasy adventure with a gritty old-school flavour.  Newcomers welcome, no system or setting familiarity required
