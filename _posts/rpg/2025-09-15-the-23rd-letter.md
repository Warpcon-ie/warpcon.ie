The 23rd Letter is a technothriller where players are a Cell in The Network, an underground organisation dedicated to protecting Espers--people with psionic abilities. Players will take on the role of Espers (or not, as they choose!) and play as the entire Cell, working together to try to complete a covert mission. Players will also receive a playtesting credit in the final printed game, the third edition of which is due out later in 2026.
