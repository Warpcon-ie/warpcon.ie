You must stop a pair of sabre-toothed cats that have been menacing the sacred cattle of a local clan. A rival clan also threatens to take the cattle! How you handle this confrontation determines whether there will be a bloody feud between two clans -- or even tribal war...

Bronze-age fantasy adventure with a gritty old-school flavour.  Newcomers welcome, no system or setting familiarity required
