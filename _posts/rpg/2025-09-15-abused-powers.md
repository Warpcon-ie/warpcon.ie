Modern day US.
"Why did you join the clearly evil cult that is actively trying to unwind reality?"
"Magical health benefits. ALL my chronic pain was literally hand waved away."
"....so are you still recruiting?"
"But exposing your mind to magic will unravel your soul, leaving you only with
despair!"
Waves vaguely at the state of the world. "That ship has fucking sailed my guy."
You are a person who chose to join a cult that kills people for supernatural powers,
because it was the best of your profoundly shitty options.
The cult's "leader" is pretty sure that 'The blood of kings' is important for some
reason, so landlords are your targets as ritualistically killing them seems to generate
the most power. Unfortunately murdering rich assholes tends to draw the attention of
law enforcement, so try to become bulletproof before that happens.
Content warnings: Violence, body horror, villain protagonists, hostile law
enforcement, abuse of power.
