The Northern Ocean Trading Company and Aragonite Royal Monopoly companies have ruled your land for the last three hundred years, exploiting your resources and subjugating your people. Now they're  vulnerable and you have an opportunity to strike back. Where once we were divided we must for the first time unite. Last of the Free Tribes, assemble and remind them who's land this is.
A political larp about fighting back against colonial oppression.
